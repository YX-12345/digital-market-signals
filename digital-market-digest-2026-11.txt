SOUTHEAST ASIA AND BRAZIL | ADVANCED-CHIP CONTROLS
A U.S. interim final rule effective November 19 adds license requirements for specified advanced chips and related items to or within Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil.
Why it matters: For affected transactions, hardware delivery and compliance can shape a project schedule even where the facility itself is ready.

INDIA | BIHAR DATA CENTER PROPOSALS
CtrlS described a proposed 150 MW facility in Bodh Gaya in October. On November 20 Reuters identified Patna in reporting a later pact for the same stated scale.
Why it matters: Resolve the site and project identity before counting these proposals in a regional supply pipeline.

NIGERIA | FEASIBILITY FUNDING
USTDA announced an agreement to fund feasibility work for two proposed facilities in Lagos and Delta states.
Why it matters: The study offers a point to assess power, demand and financing before assuming a build will proceed.
