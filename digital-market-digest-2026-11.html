Digital Market Digest — November 2026

Three developments illustrate different milestones. A rule changes licensing, a pact records an investment plan, and a grant funds a study.

Download email-ready text

Southeast Asia and Brazil · Advanced-chip controls

A U.S. rule that took effect November 19 added licensing requirements for specified advanced computing items sent to or transferred within Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brazil.

Why it matters. The requirements can affect when covered equipment reaches a data center. An operating site and a licensed hardware transaction are distinct milestones.

India · Bihar data center proposals

CtrlS’s October release named Bodh Gaya for a 150 MW proposal. On November 20, Reuters reported a pact of the same stated scale and named Patna.

Why it matters. The disclosures leave the relationship between the proposals unresolved. Counting both as new capacity would assume they are separate.

Research record · CtrlS release · Reuters update

Nigeria · Feasibility funding

The U.S. Trade and Development Agency announced a grant for feasibility work on proposed data centers in Lagos and Delta states.

Why it matters. The study brings technical and financial assessment into the public record. A decision on which sites to develop would be a later milestone.
