Three developments worth watching

  • Chip licensing adds a delivery dependency

    New U.S. licensing requirements cover specified advanced computing items in four Southeast Asian markets and Brazil.

    Equipment supply can face licensing delays even at an operating site.

    Read the export-controls signal

  • Bihar disclosures name different sites

    CtrlS’s October release cites Bodh Gaya. November reporting names Patna for a proposal of the same stated scale.

    The overlap is unclear, so summing the proposed capacity risks double counting.

    Compare the Bihar disclosures

    USTDA announced funding to study two proposed data center sites.

    The grant moves the proposals into technical and financial assessment.
