AI Infrastructure & Cloud Markets in Emerging Economies

When should emerging economies rely on cloud providers for AI compute, and when does investing in local or shared capacity make sense?

Key takeaways

  • Local cloud regions expand hosting choices. Google opened in Querétaro and Azure made Indonesia Central generally available. Workload owners still need to verify which services and accelerators are offered. Mexico · Indonesia
  • Public programs can create another route to compute. India launched a portal for subsidized access. Actual availability, allocation and cost are the questions to track. India
  • Project status matters for delivery planning. Kenya’s proposed Olkaria cloud campus illustrates how an investment announcement can precede unresolved implementation questions. Kenya
