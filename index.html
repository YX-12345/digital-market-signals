Three developments worth watching

  • Chip supply can lag site readiness. New U.S. licensing requirements cover specified advanced chips in four Southeast Asian markets and Brazil. Verify hardware delivery separately from facility opening. Read the export-controls signal
  • Investment announcements can obscure site scope. CtrlS’s October Bihar release cites Bodh Gaya, while November reporting identifies Patna for a proposal with the same stated scale. Compare the Bihar disclosures
  • Feasibility funding is an early milestone. A USTDA-funded study will assess two proposed Nigerian facilities. Power, demand and financing remain questions to investigate. Read the Nigeria signal
