Initial research focus

AI Infrastructure & Cloud Markets in Emerging Economies

What shapes where AI and cloud capacity is built, and who can access it?

Project Overview

Digital Market Signals is an independent portfolio project in development. It aims to help readers follow technology market developments and assess their relevance to digital infrastructure and access in emerging economies.

Focus Areas

  • AI infrastructure

    Where is compute capacity planned or deployed? How might power, connectivity and hardware availability shape those decisions?

  • Cloud markets

    Which providers and partnerships are expanding local service availability? What can customers deploy locally?

  • Policy and access

    How might data governance, procurement and affordability influence who can use these services?

Methodology

Proposed research and review process

  1. Capture the evidence. Record the source, date, geography, and scope for each development.
  2. Verify the claim. Check original disclosures where available, corroborate material claims, and distinguish announced projects from operating capacity.
  3. Synthesize and document. Use AI to assist with organizing and drafting where appropriate. Review claims against cited sources and disclose AI use and evidence gaps in published work.
