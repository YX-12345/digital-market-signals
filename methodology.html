Methodology

How this portfolio selects, checks, and explains public evidence about technology markets.

How developments are selected

Priority goes to developments that materially change what is known about availability, access, or project status. Useful records have specific, traceable evidence and an observable point for follow-up, such as a service listing, reported allocation, or commissioning notice. Repeated announcements earn a place when they add evidence or expose a discrepancy.

Featured research · IndiaAI reservation costs

The IndiaAI study estimates when reserving a GPU costs less than paying for use on demand. The comparison holds the configuration fixed and varies billable hours and the reservation term.

  1. Capture the posted rates. Match the same six single-GPU instance IDs in the IndiaAI price list and price calculator. Record the four rates per instance in INR per instance-hour. The November 21, 2026 snapshot preserves those 24 rates. The selection illustrates differences in pricing and has no claim to market representativeness.
  2. Model like-for-like costs. Multiply expected hours by the on-demand rate and full term hours by the reserved rate. Use the portal’s 720, 4,320 and 8,760 billed hours. Divide the reserved rate by the on-demand rate to find the share of the term where charges match. The scenario explorer excludes GST, add-ons and subsidies, and assumes on-demand availability.
  3. Check what the sources establish. Use the portal’s policy, FAQ and service agreement for access rules. Preserve each progress figure’s date, unit and stage. Listings, approvals, allocations and logged usage answer different questions. Measuring utilization would require actual hours used and available hours over the same period and scope.
  4. Update the evidence and analysis together. When listed rates change, record a new dated snapshot, check both sources and rerun the cost-model tests. Reconcile the chart, rate table and hypothetical example with the snapshot. The study’s methods and sources document the formulas, assumptions and evidence gaps.

Archived worked example · Indonesia Central

The Indonesia case uses a separate method to trace a reported market milestone from the original notice to a published signal.

  1. Original source. Microsoft’s April 15, 2025 availability notice says organizations could select Indonesia Central in Azure and identifies three availability zones.
  2. Extracted claim. Record the operator, region, date, three zones and the milestone of general availability.
  3. Verification. Compare the first release with Microsoft’s May 27 launch-event announcement. The events have different dates and describe different milestones. Both accounts are from Microsoft.
  4. Analysis. An in-country cloud region is a location that a buyer can evaluate. The notices supply no customer-level GPU quotas, measured latency or utilization.
  5. Published record. The Indonesia Central signal keeps April 15 as the original source date, records the May event in its history and shows the limitations alongside the analysis.

Repository review checklist

How records are maintained and corrected

The Evidence Library sorts by the original source’s publication date. The milestone date describes the event or notice named on the record. A last substantive update marks a dated change documented in the history, including attributed reporting where identified. Last reviewed records when the sources were checked.

Milestones are grouped by cloud availability, infrastructure investment, regulation and compute service. A study grant, signed agreement and operating facility represent distinct evidence. Reference records keep earlier milestones accessible for comparison. Featured updates are editorial selections based on the significance of the change and the evidence available.

  1. Find or add the record in signals-data.js. Keep the record ID and original source publication date stable.
  2. Add the follow-up source, with its type and date. Log the dated change in the record history and distinguish an observed milestone from a reported or planned one. Change the last-reviewed date after the source check.
  3. Correct affected text in the record and any related homepage insight, brief, digest or comparison. Describe material changes in the record history and repository commit.
  4. Preview search, geography, links and any amended data. After publishing, reopen the live record and confirm the dates, sources and status.

Evidence labels

Source-checked fact
A statement supported within the scope of the cited document, such as a published rule or a documented study agreement.
Operator or government claim
An attributed statement from the party making it, including reported availability and announced targets.
Analysis
The project’s interpretation of why a development could matter or what a practitioner should verify before acting.
Uncertainty
Missing, provisional or disputed evidence, with the next check stated where possible.
