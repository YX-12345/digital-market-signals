Methodology

How this portfolio selects, checks, and explains public evidence about technology markets.

How developments are selected

Priority goes to developments that materially change what is known about availability, access, or project status. Useful records have specific, traceable evidence and an observable point for follow-up, such as a service listing, reported allocation, or commissioning notice. Repeated announcements earn a place when they add evidence or expose a discrepancy.

Worked example · Indonesia Central

  1. Original source. Microsoft’s April 15, 2025 availability notice says organizations could select Indonesia Central in Azure and identifies three availability zones.
  2. Extracted claim. Record the operator, region, date, three zones and the milestone of general availability.
  3. Verification. Compare the first release with Microsoft’s May 27 launch-event announcement. The events have different dates and describe different milestones. Both accounts are from Microsoft.
  4. Analysis. An in-country cloud region is a location that a buyer can evaluate. The notices supply no customer-level GPU quotas, measured latency or utilization.
  5. Published record. The Indonesia Central signal keeps April 15 as the original source date, records the May event in its history and shows the limitations alongside the analysis.

Sources and AI assistance

OpenAI Codex assisted with finding and organizing sources, checking claims against cited documents and data, drafting text, preparing data and building the site.

Records link to original sources, identify who made each claim and retain conflicts or gaps. “Last reviewed” is the date of the most recent source check.

Repository review checklist

How records are maintained and corrected

  1. Find or add the record in signals-data.js. Keep the record ID and original source publication date stable.
  2. Add the follow-up source, with its type and date. Log the dated change in the record history and distinguish an observed milestone from a reported or planned one. Change the last-reviewed date after the source check.
  3. Correct affected text in the record and any related homepage insight, brief, digest or comparison. Describe material changes in the record history and repository commit.
  4. Preview search, geography, links and any amended data. After publishing, reopen the live record and confirm the dates, sources and status.

Evidence labels

Source-checked fact
A statement supported within the scope of the cited document, such as a published rule or a documented study agreement.
Operator or government claim
An attributed statement from the party making it, including reported availability and announced targets.
Analysis
The project’s interpretation of why a development could matter or what a practitioner should verify before acting.
Uncertainty
Missing, provisional or disputed evidence, with the next check stated where possible.
