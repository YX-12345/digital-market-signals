Featured Updates

Export controls

U.S. chip rule adds licensing requirements in five markets

Rule effective Nov 19, 2026 · Federal Register

The U.S. rule added licensing requirements for covered items in five markets and separate end-use controls involving certain remote access to compute.

Why it matters. An effect on hardware delivery or cloud services depends on the items, parties and transaction.

Next check. BIS guidance and evidence of changes to covered shipments or named services.

Data center investment

Bihar’s AI data center proposal moves from letter of intent to MoU

MoU signed Nov 20, 2026 · CtrlS

CtrlS linked the November MoU to its October letter of intent for a proposed 150 MW facility and identified the Patna area as its preferred location.

Why it matters. The releases describe one evolving proposal. CtrlS separately reported the inauguration of a 12 MW Patna facility.

Next check. Final site, financing and construction milestones.

Cloud availability

AWS opens its Chile region to selected customers

Early access announced Nov 11, 2026 · About Amazon

AWS reported limited early access and expected general availability by year-end. It gave a separate timetable for Bedrock and SageMaker AI.

Why it matters. Eligibility to use a region and availability of a specific AI service are separate milestones.

Next check. A dated general-availability notice and the regional service catalog.

When can customers use a new cloud region for specific AI workloads?

These announcements document different stages of regional availability. Each AI workload still needs a service or instance check in the region where it would run.

AWS Chile
Limited early access. AWS reported access for selected customers on November 11, 2026.
Evidence needed. A general availability notice and regional listings for Bedrock and SageMaker AI.
Alibaba Cloud Brazil
Core cloud services launched. Alibaba Cloud listed core services and described its agentic AI suite as planned on August 27, 2026.
Evidence needed. A dated Brazil catalog for named AI products and accelerator instances.
Azure Indonesia
General availability. Microsoft said customers could select Indonesia Central on April 15, 2025.
Evidence needed. Regional GPU instance listings and the quotas available to customers.
