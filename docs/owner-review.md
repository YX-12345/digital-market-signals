# Owner review checklist

Use this checklist to review a material claim or dataset update. Record only the checks completed and their scope.

1. Open the original document or dataset. Record the publisher, publication and effective dates where relevant, the exact claim checked, and the location of supporting evidence.
2. Verify the geography, project identity, legal or technical scope, and milestone. Distinguish an operator’s report from an observed event and from the portfolio’s interpretation. Record any conflicts or unresolved questions.
3. Recompute any changed figures from the committed data. Check keys, reference years, units, missing observations, rounding, and whether the denominator or coverage changed.
4. Record the reviewer, date, record or page ID, sources, changes and outcome. Carry corrections through related records, the brief, dashboard notes and both digest formats where applicable.
5. Verify the updated live URLs, links and relevant interactions after publication.
